Lunar PBR maps for foreground_planet.glb
Source: Poly Haven Moon 01, https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_01
License: CC0.
Diffuse, OpenGL normal, and AO/roughness/metalness source JPEG maps were optimized locally into WebP.
The hero also uses a restrained seamless albedo generated from `generate-albedo.py` to add lunar dust variation and small impact bowls to the low-contrast diffuse map. It remains a local project asset; the original Poly Haven material maps are preserved.
